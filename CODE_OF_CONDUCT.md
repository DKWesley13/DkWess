# Community conduct

SecureRepo welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, documentation improvements, tests, and new ideas.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, avoid harassment or threats, avoid posting credentials/private data, disclose security-sensitive context when relevant, and follow maintainer requests intended to keep discussions safe and on topic.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions or discussions that materially violate these expectations.

For security-sensitive matters, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of posting details publicly.
